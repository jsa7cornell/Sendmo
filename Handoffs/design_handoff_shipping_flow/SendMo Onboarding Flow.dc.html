Four skippable questions, one honest progress bar

Every question in this flow — destination, origin, package, shipping — can be answered or handed to the sender. Answer everything and you buy a label now, at an exact price. Hand anything off and you get a shipping link instead, and you pay when it's used. Nothing below asks the user to pick "label" or "link" up front — that choice is just what their answers add up to.

The progress mechanism

One bar, four segments, always — Destination, Origin, Package, Shipping. A segment fills solid when answered, and turns amber when skipped. A trailing pill names the destination the answers are building toward, and it morphs the instant a skip changes that destination. The bar never swaps to a different shape between paths — it just fills in differently.

On defaults: the answer lives in a two-way toggle, not a radio hidden under a form — "I have it" and "Sender fills this in" sit side by side, equal weight, above the fields. Toggling to "Sender fills this in" doesn't swap the screen for a big colored panel — it just dims the fields in place, so the layout never jumps. Nothing is pre-selected: whichever you tap is the answer.

What to notice

  • A two-way toggle — "I have it" / "Sender fills this in" — replaces the old skip card. Toggling back to "I have it" is the undo — no separate control needed.
  • Handing off doesn't swap the screen for a big colored panel — the fields just dim in place, so nothing jumps.
  • Email and Google sign-in live on their own step, right before payment — not squeezed into the first address form.
  • The first hand-off shows a one-time explainer bubble, then gets out of the way.
  • The progress bar just fills in and highlights the current numbered step — no checkmarks or arrows to parse.

What to notice

  • Only the fields the creator actually deferred show up here — never a field that was already answered.
  • The destination card only shows city/state, or a name the sender typed themselves — never a street address (Rule 7).
  • Rates show no prices, just a rough cost tier ($–$$$$) and a "Preferred by Jordan" badge — the sender never sees what Jordan pays.
  • The finale never mentions cost — "Jordan already paid for shipping" is the only money language the sender sees.

Motion notes

  • Step transitions — keep the app's existing pattern: 250ms ease, 20px horizontal slide + fade, AnimatePresence mode="wait". No new timing needed.
  • Progress node morph — 300ms color/background/border crossfade on the node itself (already just CSS transitions, no JS animation needed) plus a brief scale pulse (1 → 1.08 → 1, spring) on the exact node that flips to skipped, so the eye catches which question just changed.
  • Outcome pill — crossfades background, border and text together over 300–350ms; pair with a 4px upward shift so it reads as a gentle flip rather than a flicker.
  • Skip banner — height + opacity animate in/out (Framer's height:"auto" pattern, already used for validation errors in the codebase). Same easing on undo, reversed.
  • Guestimator success — keep the existing spring checkmark pop (stiffness 400, damping 24) — it already works well, no change.
