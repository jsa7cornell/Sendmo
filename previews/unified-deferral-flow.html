Idea 1 — the separate path disappears. Fill everything in and it is an ordinary label; skip something and it's a link. So /onboarding/full-label/… vs /onboarding/flexible/… stops being a fork the user walks and becomes a value we compute at the end. One step map, one set of guards, one flow to test.
Idea 2 — flag on the payer, not the shape. Skipping the recipient address gives the other party control of the destination while you still pay. That's the seller link's shape with the opposite payer. Real product ("I'll ship this wherever you want it" — gifts, prizes, "send it to your new place"), but it must never be described as the seller flow, where the buyer pays. Who-pays confusion is what the seller-link review spent most of its time on.
